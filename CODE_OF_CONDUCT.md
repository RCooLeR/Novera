# Community conduct

Novera contributors and maintainers are expected to keep project spaces safe,
professional, and focused on the work.

Be respectful, assume good faith while evaluating evidence, give actionable
technical feedback, and protect private or security-sensitive information. Do
not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish another person's private data, or
use project channels for unrelated commercial promotion.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and may restrict
participation when necessary. Report ordinary conduct concerns privately to a
repository maintainer through GitHub. Report security vulnerabilities through
the process in `SECURITY.md`; never include exploit details in a public conduct
report.

Enforcement decisions should be documented privately, proportionate, and free
from retaliation. If the project establishes a formal foundation or community
governance model, this policy should be replaced with a named, versioned code of
conduct and a monitored independent contact route.
